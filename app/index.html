⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. The balcony meditation with Lucia on the morning after the mission ‘Down to Earth’, before you leave Chapter 1, cannot be repeated: there is no mission replay. Do it before Chapter 1 ends and keep a manual save. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.
